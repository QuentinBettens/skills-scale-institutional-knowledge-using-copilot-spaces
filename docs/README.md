# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
This repository contains the OctoAcme project management guidance used to initiate, plan, execute, release, and improve cross-functional work. The process is intentionally lightweight but structured: teams validate the business problem, define success metrics and stakeholders, then turn approved ideas into a plan with clear milestones, ownership, and dependencies. These documents provide a central reference for the lifecycle of work from concept to delivery and continuous improvement.

## Project Management Process Summary
OctoAcme follows a project model centered on customer value, measurable outcomes, and iterative delivery. Projects begin with initiation and validation, where a one-pager captures the problem, goals, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and resource needs. Once approved, planning breaks work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, and a Definition of Done so teams can deliver shippable increments without losing alignment on scope or quality. Throughout execution, teams use daily communication, milestone reviews, and risk tracking to manage dependencies and keep delivery visible to stakeholders.

The operating model depends on clear roles and strong collaboration. Product managers define the what and why by prioritizing outcomes and success metrics, while project managers coordinate schedules, milestones, risks, and communication. Developers implement the work and maintain quality through tests, reviews, and technical execution, and QA helps validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release. This shared ownership model helps reduce ambiguity, supports accountability, and keeps teams focused on customer and business value.

Communication is treated as a core delivery practice rather than a side activity. OctoAcme recommends weekly PM and product syncs, team standups, milestone updates, and escalation paths when blockers or cross-team dependencies emerge. Risks and issues are tracked in a simple register and reviewed regularly so stakeholders receive consistent updates and can make timely decisions. Quality assurance is embedded across the lifecycle through unit, integration, and end-to-end testing where applicable, security checks in CI, manual QA for acceptance validation, and release readiness gates such as smoke tests and rollback planning. Together, these practices create a disciplined but adaptable process that emphasizes transparency, quality, and continuous improvement.
